molt-DEX is for reverse engineering and security assessment that you are **authorized** to
perform: your own apps, targets explicitly in scope of a bug-bounty program, systems covered by a
written penetration-test agreement, CTF challenges, and deliberately-vulnerable training apps.

written permission to test. Reverse engineering and security testing may be restricted by an
